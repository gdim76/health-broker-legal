# Health Broker User Agreement

**Effective date: 26 November 2026**

## Purpose

Health Broker is a personal Android proof of concept. It reads supported data
from a Xiaomi Mi Body Composition Scale 2 and may, after separate Huawei Health
authorization, write a user-confirmed weight measurement to Huawei Health.

## Your responsibilities

You are responsible for reviewing each measurement before confirming that it is
yours. A matching weight range is only a weak filter and does not establish
identity. Do not confirm or synchronize a measurement that belongs to another
person.

## Health and safety notice

Health Broker is not a medical device and does not provide diagnosis,
treatment, medical advice, or emergency services. Weight, impedance, and any
future calculated values may be inaccurate. Do not make medical decisions based
on the app. Consult a qualified healthcare professional for health concerns.

## Third-party services

Bluetooth devices, Zepp, Huawei ID, Huawei Health, HMS Core, and AppGallery
Connect are third-party services subject to their own terms and privacy
policies. Huawei Health synchronization remains unavailable unless Huawei grants
the necessary scope and you authorize it.
